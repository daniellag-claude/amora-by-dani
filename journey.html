My journey

How Amora came to be

Part perfume, part chaos, all heart. Here is the slightly messy, very fun story of a girl figuring out the business world one bottle at a time.

Me on a typical day: one part inspiration, two parts figuring it out.

Where it started

One idea, zero clue, lots of heart

I did not wake up one day knowing how to run a business. Honestly, I still google half of it. What I did have was one stubborn little idea: that a perfume should feel like you, not just smell nice.

So I rolled up my sleeves in Johannesburg, made a glorious mess, tested far too many blends, and slowly turned that idea into something real. Amora is me learning the business world out loud, and loving every chaotic minute of it.

The story so far

From idea to bottle

1

The lightbulb moment

It started with one cheeky thought: why can't a perfume have a personality, a mood, a whole vibe of its own? Once I thought it, I could not unthink it.

2

Trial, error and a lot of sniffing

Cue months of blending, testing, getting it gloriously wrong, and trying again. Three personalities finally stuck: the Workoholic, the Overthinker and the Charmer.

3

Welcome to the business world

Then came the grown-up stuff: pricing, packaging, labels, and a hundred things I learned on the fly. Turns out running a brand is a whole adventure of its own.

4

Making it unmistakably mine

I gave every scent its own playlist on a little QR card, because a mood is never only a smell. Hand-blended in small batches in Joburg, with a personal hello in every order.

What's next

Still figuring it out, still having fun

I learn something new every single day, and this is only chapter one. Grab the scent that sounds like you and come along for the ride.
