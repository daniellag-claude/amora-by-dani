Our story

Scent, meet sound

A small, handmade perfume label from Johannesburg, built on one belief: a scent should feel like you.

Meet Dani

Hi, I'm Dani, the heart behind Amora

Amora started with one simple belief: a perfume should feel like you, not just smell nice. That is why I built each scent around a personality. The Workoholic is for the ones who never stop, the Overthinker is for the busy minds, and the Charmer is for easy, effortless confidence.

Because a mood is never only a smell, every scent comes with its own playlist tucked inside your order. You spray it on, scan the code, and the feeling is complete.

Each one is hand-blended into a 15ml spray and made with love, right here in Johannesburg.
